## Seven-day zero-budget launch

1. Day 1: Publish the LinkedIn post with the portrait creative.
2. Day 2: Record a 20-second screen video completing one question and showing the score.
3. Day 3: Share in two relevant college or placement WhatsApp groups with moderator permission.
4. Day 4: Post the feedback-first Reddit version in one relevant community after checking its self-promotion rules.
5. Day 5: Publish one carousel: weak introduction vs evidence-based introduction.
6. Day 6: Ask five students directly to test it while screen sharing; record where they hesitate.
7. Day 7: Compare `/source/*`, `/completed/*`, and `/full-interview/*` counts in admin monitoring before posting again.

Do not spam identical captions across communities. A useful answer or resource
should be the main content; the challenge link is the optional next step.
